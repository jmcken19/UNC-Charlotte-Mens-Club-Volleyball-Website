Join the team

Come play with us

New to the program? Here's how to go from interested to on the roster.

  1. Join us on NinerEngage

    Follow the club on NinerEngage to get team announcements, tryout dates and contact info.

    Open NinerEngage

  2. Come to practice

    We practice Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 6 to 9 PM at the Student Activities Center. Bring court shoes, kneepads and water.

  3. Talk to the team

    Ask any player or the coach at practice about tryouts and what it takes to make the roster.

Questions

Do I need experience?

Some experience helps, since we're a competitive team, but plenty of our players picked the sport up late. Come to a practice and see.

When is the season?

Fall is tournament season, traveling to other universities. Spring is ECVA league play, ending with Nationals.

Where do you play?

Practices are at the Student Activities Center (SAC) on campus. Tournaments are across North Carolina, the Southeast and beyond.

What positions do you need?

Every season is different. Check the roster to see who we have, then tell us what you play when you come out.

UNC Charlotte Men's Club Volleyball

A competitive, traveling club team in Division II of the Eastern Collegiate Volleyball Association. Practices Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 6–9 PM at the Student Activities Center.
