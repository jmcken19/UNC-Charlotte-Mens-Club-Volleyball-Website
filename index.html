Who we are

Built to compete.
Built to travel.

Men's volleyball gets less spotlight than it deserves, so we bring it ourselves. The club practices hard, plays at a high level, and competes against top collegiate men's club programs, including Liberty University and 2022 national champions Virginia Tech.

  • Spring: league playWe compete in the ECVA during the spring semester.
  • Fall: tournament seasonWe travel the East Coast for tournaments hosted by other universities.
  • Open to new playersExperienced or still learning, if you want to compete, come find us.
