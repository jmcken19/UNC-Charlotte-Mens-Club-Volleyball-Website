Built to compete.
Built to travel.

The team practices regularly and plays at a high level, competing against top-ranking collegiate men's volleyball club teams around North Carolina, the Southeast, and all over the nation.

League play

Our team competes in Division II of the Eastern Collegiate Volleyball Association. We play against teams like Liberty University and the 2022 national champions, Virginia Tech.

Spring and fall

We only compete in our league during the spring semester, but travel across the East Coast during the fall semester to compete in tournaments hosted by other universities.

Practices

We practice Monday, Wednesday, and Friday 6–9 at the Student Activities Center (SAC).
